# 🏥 Executive Healthcare Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed for executive leadership to monitor physician performance, emergency blood demand, patient admission types, and clinical medication stability.

---

![Healthcare Dashboard Overview](./dashboard-overview.png)

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Insights

* **Executive KPIs:** Real-time tracking of Total Patients, Revenue, and Average Length of Stay (LOS).
* **Admission Types:** Categorization of Elective, Urgent, and Emergency cases.
* **Medication Stability Rates:** Evaluation of top condition-medication efficacy ratios.
* **Emergency Blood Demand:** Custom-sorted distribution of blood types needed for critical cases.
* **Detailed Clinical Table:** Patient-level records with clean alignment and custom DAX metrics.
